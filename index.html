THE ATELIER

A Dialogue Between Space, Light & Material

Founded in Lahore, PAK Interiors designs private residences that favor stillness over noise. We believe architecture is not merely shelter, but a frame for living in quiet grace.

Each commission unites modern architectural discipline with Pakistani craftsmanship. In our Gulberg studio and joinery workshop, architects and master woodworkers sculpt raw teak, Italian travertine, and smoked oak into timeless environments.
